# Claims

Use this artifact only when requested, required by the project, or when publishable proof/material externally checkable claims need durable traceability across surfaces or runs. Inline evidence is valid for bounded work; routine labels and directly inspectable task/state instructions may stay cited beside the draft.

## Policy

Only exact wording marked `publish` may appear as factual copy. Use `qualify` only after replacing the public wording with the supported narrower version. `internal`, `unknown`, and `reject` content must not ship as fact.
