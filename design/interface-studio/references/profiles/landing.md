# Public site and conversion landing profile

Load this existing profile for a new or materially redesigned public surface after classifying its primary job as `PUBLIC_CONTENT_NAVIGATION_SITE` or `CONVERSION_LANDING`. A targeted copy or direction task may use only its relevant section instead of running the full profile. Do not force public documentation, editorial, institutional, portfolio, or browse/navigation work through a conversion funnel.

## Compact working truth

Capture only what can change the requested outcome:

- source-backed product truth and unknowns;
- audience, traffic/awareness context, switching trigger, and desired action;
- real product mechanism and defensible difference;
- objections, available proof, claim IDs when durable traceability is needed, brand/assets, and technical constraints;
- decision owner when a real choice exists.

Inline notes are valid. Persist a brief only when requested, needed across runs/agents, or required by the project. Do not invent a persona or ask for facts the repository already provides.

## Preserve the classified public-surface job

- **`PUBLIC_CONTENT_NAVIGATION_SITE`** — optimize for finding, browsing, understanding, and moving among destinations. Establish audience and content scope, navigation model, page hierarchy, search/browse needs, reading order, cross-links, continuity with existing URLs/content, and the next relevant destination. A page may have actions, but do not manufacture one dominant conversion CTA, objection sequence, urgency, or funnel when the actual job is orientation or reading.
- **`CONVERSION_LANDING`** — optimize for a focused evaluation and action. Establish awareness/traffic context, proposition and mechanism, supported proof, objections, CTA consequence, form/checkout/contact path, and post-action state. Do not let persuasion erase navigation, consent, eligibility, or factual caveats.

When a route contains both jobs, identify which job governs each region and keep navigation/findability requirements visible rather than treating them as conversion friction. The classification changes priorities; it does not prescribe a fixed section count, artifact, or concept quota.

## Storyboard before prose when sequence is open

Create only as many beats as the reader's navigation/understanding path or visitor's conversion decision needs. For each beat record:

| Beat | Reader/visitor question, destination, or tension | Answer | Proof/content | Action or next destination | Transition |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Skip a new storyboard for a bounded copy patch whose placement and sequence are already governed. The first viewport should express the page thesis rather than interchangeable marketing language, but no stock section order is required.

## Copy routes only for a real strategic trade-off

If constraints select one argument, draft one complete route and proceed. If material alternatives remain, draft enough distinct routes to expose the decision. Each route should make a different strategic bet in the relevant dimensions:

- audience tension and awareness assumption;
- product mechanism and benefit chain;
- proof strategy and objection handling;
- narrative order and CTA promise;
- allowed claim evidence, gaps, and honest trade-off.

A route fails when it differs only in headline, adjective, metaphor, or tone. Do not blend alternatives before deciding, and do not create every copy × visual combination. Couple a copy route to the direction it materially supports.

## Inspiration only where evidence is missing

Retain the smallest primary-source set that unlocks the decision; no fixed count or role quota applies. Record:

```text
URL + exact artifact → observation → principle → product-specific adaptation
→ licence/reuse status → no-copy boundary
```

Separate observed evidence from inference. Stop when another reference would add taste fragments rather than decision value.

## Visual directions only for a real visual trade-off

One direction is valid when the brief, inherited system, or delegated judgment selects it. When alternatives are warranted, make each an integrated concept rather than a skin. For every direction record only applicable details:

- bound copy route or governing content;
- visual thesis and product/subject-world signature;
- topology/rhythm, typography, density, palette/asset language, and motion posture;
- readable low-fi at the viewport/state that exposes the decision, plus contrasting evidence only where behavior changes;
- implementation/accessibility risk, trade-off, and concept-specific no-go.

Do not force a GPU direction. All concepts may use semantic HTML/CSS/SVG/image/video.

## Selection and first slice

Compare only the viable rendered concepts and choose once. If the user delegated judgment, record the evidence-based choice and continue; use a fresh reviewer only when it materially reduces decision risk. If one route was already determined, mark selection `not-applicable` or direct and build.

Build the smallest slice that can validate the thesis and conversion path. Capture the affected viewport/state evidence at readable scale. Additional widths, lower sections, or states are required only when the implementation or completion claim can differ there.

## Conditional real-time graphics

Load `../realtime-graphics.md` only after an explicit user request or a named product need demonstrates why spatial/procedural rendering is material. Semantic DOM and a static poster/fallback remain the default first implementation. The active brief or inline contract must preserve, as applicable:

- canvas role and named product beat;
- remove-canvas result and equivalent semantic DOM/content/action;
- DOM/canvas/scroll/input/resource ownership and exact existing renderer versions;
- static/reduced-motion and reduced-data behavior;
- no-GPU, initialization/asset/shader failure, context/device loss, mobile/touch behavior;
- project/device-specific budgets, offscreen/hidden pause, navigation/unmount, and cleanup;
- deterministic evidence for only the keyframes/states needed by the claim.

There is no permission to install a renderer, decoder, model package, or smooth-scroll dependency. Route framework details to `vgpu` or other official relevant skills/docs. Essential text, proof, forms, focus order, anchors, and CTA never live only in the canvas.

## Public-surface review priorities

Choose from these priorities according to the classified job and review scope; a full-surface review covers its material set, while a targeted review names exclusions:

- product, content scope, and audience specificity;
- for public content/navigation, findability, route identity, navigation/current-location truth, reading hierarchy, destination clarity, and URL/content continuity;
- for conversion landing, CTA hierarchy, eligibility, consequence, and next-step truth;
- source-backed proof and claim scope;
- selected thesis/signature in the affected composition;
- nonrepetitive hierarchy appropriate to unequal content jobs;
- applicable responsive wrapping/order, forms, focus, and media;
- honest synthetic/placeholder labels and absence of invented proof;
- optional canvas value, semantic fallback, reduced modes, failure, input, pause, performance, and cleanup;
- metadata, crawl/index, consent, deployment, and publication claims kept within engineering/runtime evidence and authority.
